Accessibility In Tech

Across Africa, many digital products and platforms leave people with disabilities out. Rarely is that on purpose. Accessibility simply was not part of the plan from the beginning.

Build Without Barriers is a monthly conversation exploring how we can make different industries more inclusive, one sector at a time. We’re starting with tech because so much of our everyday lives depends on digital products. From the apps we use to the websites we visit, accessibility shapes who can use them and how easily they can do so. Our goal is to move beyond awareness and explore practical ways to make various sectors work for more people. You don’t have to be an accessibility expert to take part. You just need to be curious about what can be done better.
